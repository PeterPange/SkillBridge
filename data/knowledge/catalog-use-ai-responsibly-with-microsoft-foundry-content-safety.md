# Use AI responsibly with Microsoft Foundry Content Safety

As the amount of user-generated online content increases, so does the need to ensure harmful material is moderated effectively. Microsoft Foundry Content Safety resource includes features to help organizations moderate and manage both user-generated and AI-generated content.
